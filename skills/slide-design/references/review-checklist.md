# Review checklist

Look at the render (`decks/<slug>/render/slide-NN.png` or `render/sheet.png`) with your image-viewing tool
before you show a slide. Fix what fails, then show it; do not polish beyond this list.

- **Message:** the slide says one thing, and a glance at the structure says it before the text does. The
  title (if there is one) states the takeaway, not a topic.
- **Nothing clipped:** no text runs out of its shape or off the slide, no line breaks in the middle of a
  word, no label wrapping where a wider box would fit. The render can break lines a little differently from
  PowerPoint, so a box filled to its last line needs more room.
- **Readable:** light text only on strong fills, dark text on light ones; nothing tiny.
- **What should align, aligns:** edges of shapes that belong together line up, equal things have equal
  sizes and gaps. Deliberate asymmetry is fine.
- **The two hard rules:** no keyword chains joined by " · ", " | " or " • "; no paragraph over three lines.
- **Feedback addressed:** every point the user raised is visibly handled, and nothing else changed. With a
  screenshot, compare it with the new render side by side.
